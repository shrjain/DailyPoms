Privacy Policy

Daily Poms is an offline, on-device app. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • All focus sessions, labels, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone (and included in your personal Apple device backups if enabled).
  • Deleting the app or tapping Settings → Erase All Data removes your data from the device.
