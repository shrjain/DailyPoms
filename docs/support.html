Support & Help

Daily Poms tracks focus chunks on screen and counts your daily poms toward a daily target.

Tips

  • Use the 15m / 25m / 45m / 60m pills on the Today tab to pick a chunk length before starting.
  • Leave Keep screen awake while running enabled in Settings if you keep your iPhone on your desk as a visual timer.
  • Use Log a finished chunk on the Today tab to record a session you did away from your phone.
